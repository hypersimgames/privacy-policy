Privacy Policy

Developer / Studio: Hypersim
Effective Date: October 18, 2026

Hypersim ("we," "our," or "us") develops and publishes mobile games and applications ("Services"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you download, install, and play our games available on the Google Play Store and other platforms.

By using our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

We do not require you to create a direct account or provide personal details (such as your real name, physical address, or phone number) to play our games. However, our applications integrate third-party software development kits (SDKs) for advertising, in-app purchases, analytics, and core game functionality, which automatically collect certain device and usage data.

A. Automatically Collected Data

When you access our games, third-party services integrated into the app may automatically collect:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID), App Set ID, or similar unique device identifiers used for advertising, fraud prevention, and analytics.
  • Device and Network Information: Device model, operating system version, language settings, screen resolution, mobile carrier, and IP address (used to estimate general, coarse geographical location such as country or city).
  • App Activity and Gameplay Data: Session duration, level progression, in-game interactions, ad views/clicks, and gameplay telemetry.
  • Diagnostics and Crash Logs: Crash reports, frame rate data, memory usage, and performance logs to identify bugs and improve stability.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our games use Google AdMob (and potentially its mediation partners) to display advertisements, such as banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID, coarse location (derived from your IP address), and app interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, cap ad frequency, and combat fraud.

  • Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can opt out of interest-based advertising or reset your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device settings by navigating to Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) and selecting "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization."
  • Where required by law (such as in the EEA/UK), we request your consent before serving personalized advertisements.

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Our games may offer virtual items, currency, ad-removal upgrades, or premium features via In-App Purchases (IAP). All financial transactions are processed directly and securely by Google Play Billing.

  • No Access to Payment Details: Hypersim does not collect, receive, process, or store your credit card numbers, bank account details, or billing addresses.
  • Transaction Verification: We only receive non-sensitive confirmation data from Google Play (such as an order ID, purchase token, and product ID) to verify your purchase, unlock in-game content, and restore non-consumable purchases across devices.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We share or allow direct collection of data by the following trusted third-party service providers solely to operate, monetize, and improve our games. Please review their respective privacy policies for detailed information on how they handle your data:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Games Services (if leaderboards, achievements, or cloud saves are enabled)
  • Unity Technologies (game engine runtime, hardware telemetry, Unity Analytics, and Unity Ads)
  • Google Firebase / Crashlytics (if crash reporting or analytics are active)

5. Data Security

All data transmitted between our games and third-party services (such as AdMob, Google Play Services, and Unity) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). While we rely on trusted platform providers to safeguard information, please be aware that no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Game Data: Game progress, settings, and preferences stored locally on your device remain there until you clear the app's storage cache or uninstall the game.
  • Third-Party Data: Data collected by third-party SDKs (such as Google AdMob and Unity) is retained and deleted in accordance with their respective data retention policies.
  • Requesting Data Deletion: Because we do not maintain our own user account servers or store personally identifiable data on proprietary databases, you can clear local data by uninstalling the app and resetting your Advertising ID in your device settings. If you have questions or wish to request deletion of any specific identifier associated with support inquiries, please contact us at the email address below.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our Services are intended for a general audience and are not directed toward children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction, such as 16 in certain European countries). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. Where applicable, our ad networks are configured to comply with child-directed treatment policies. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately so that we can delete the information.

8. International Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location (including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Brazil, or California), you may have the right to:

  • Request access to or information about the categories of data collected about your device.
  • Withdraw consent for personalized advertising at any time via in-app consent prompts or device settings.
  • Request deletion of personal data where applicable.
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our games, third-party SDKs, or legal requirements. We will post any changes on this page and update the "Effective Date" at the top. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
